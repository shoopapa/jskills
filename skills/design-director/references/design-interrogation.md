# Before delivery

Use these questions to find concrete defects, not to generate a long ceremonial checklist. Fix the highest-impact issue first.

## Purpose and hierarchy
- Can the intended reader tell what this is, why it matters, and what to do next in a few seconds?
- What gets noticed first? Is that the right thing? Is the primary action distinguishable from secondary actions?
- Does each section earn its place? Can repeated explanation, filler copy, or redundant framing be removed?

## Typography
- Does the scale distinguish title, section, body, labels, and notes without excessive font sizes or weights?
- Are line lengths comfortable? Do line breaks support meaning at both narrow and wide sizes?
- Are numerals aligned, units explicit, labels readable, and dense data still usable?
- Are font choices appropriate and available in the actual export or deployment environment?

## Color and composition
- Does color have a defined role? Does the design still make sense in grayscale?
- Are alignments shared deliberately? Do spacing and margins reveal grouping and reading order?
- Are cards, borders, shadows, gradients, and icons doing a job, or disguising weak structure?
- Is visual variety serving content rather than making every section feel unrelated?

## Medium and functionality
- Web: test keyboard focus, empty/error/success states, long content, downloads, stale results, and narrow screens. Check overflow and tap targets.
- Slides: inspect at presentation size; check narrative sequence, chart labels, notes, and editable source where requested.
- PDFs/reports: inspect every rendered page for breaks, clipped content, repeated headers, captions, and print legibility.
- Spreadsheets: check calculations, input/formula distinction, number formats, freeze/filter behavior, error states, and print areas.
- Charts: are the units, time range, source, baseline, scale, and uncertainty honest and clear? Does the chart support a real comparison?

## Final craft
- Would this layout still make sense if the brand name were removed, or is it an interchangeable template?
- Which single removal would make it clearer? Which single detail would make it more useful?
- Have changes been inspected in the actual rendered result, rather than only in code?
